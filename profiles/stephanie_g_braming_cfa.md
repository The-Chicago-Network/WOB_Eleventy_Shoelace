bio: Stephanie Braming is a proven board director, investment management chief executive and institutional investor with over 30 years of experience in governance, leadership, strategy, financial oversight and global equity investing. She currently serves as an Independent Trustee of the State Street Mutual Funds. As the former Chief Executive and Global Head of William Blair Investment Management, Ms. Braming led enterprise strategy, growth and financial performance. She also served on the governing Executive Committee of William Blair. Ms. Braming brings a differentiated institutional investor lens to the boardroom, aligning strategy, governance and reporting with the expectations of sophisticated capital providers. She is an Audit Committee Qualified Financial Expert, providing rigorous, investor-informed oversight across financial reporting, enterprise risk, capital allocation and executive compensation. <br /><br />Ms. Braming’s board and governance expertise spans investment funds, operating company leadership and nonprofits, with experience across strategy, financial performance, risk oversight and executive compensation. She currently serves as an Independent Trustee of the State Street Mutual Funds, providing fiduciary and governance oversight of the fund complex. She is a Trustee of DePauw University and a member of the Investment Committee and Business &amp; Financial Affairs Committee. She is a Trustee of the Griffin Museum of Science &amp; Industry, where she is Audit Committee Vice Chair and a Finance Committee member. Previously, she was on the Nominating &amp; Governance Committee. She serves on the Board and Finance Committee of Working in the Schools.<br /><br />Previously, Ms. Braming served as Chair of the William Blair Funds and Luxembourg-domiciled SICAVs, providing fiduciary oversight and governance across multiple jurisdictions. She was a member of the governing Executive Committee of William Blair, with responsibility for firmwide strategy, financial performance, risk oversight and executive compensation. Ms. Braming also recently served on the Board of Governors of the Investment Company Institute.<br /><br />As Chief Executive and Global Head of William Blair Investment Management, Ms. Braming led enterprise strategy, financial performance, investments, risk, infrastructure and global distribution. She drove international expansion through acquisitions and lift-outs and enhanced global distribution, while strengthening investment expertise and firmwide infrastructure.<br /><br />Previously, Ms. Braming managed $19 billion in institutional international equity portfolios. In this role, she drove investment decision-making spanning global sectors, engaged with company management teams, and led investment teams to deliver investment results. She was formerly a principal at Mercer Investment Consulting, advising institutions on asset allocation and manager selection. She began her career at the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago.<br /><br />Ms. Braming received an MBA from the University of Chicago Booth School of Business and a BA from DePauw University. She is a CFA charterholder and holds the NACD Directorship Certification. Ms. Braming has been recognized by Forbes, Barron’s, Pensions &amp; Investments, American Banker, and Crain’s for her leadership. In 2025, she received the CFA Society Chicago’s Hortense Friedman Award for Excellence.

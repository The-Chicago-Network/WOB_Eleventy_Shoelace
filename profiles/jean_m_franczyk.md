bio: Jean M. Franczyk is an experienced non-profit chief executive, leading
  complex organizational mergers, redesigning operational teams, driving
  double-digit revenue growth, and managing large governing and philanthropic
  boards through opportunity and risk. She is both strategic and pragmatic,
  inspiring teams to operational excellence while positioning organizations for
  long-term growth and change. <br /><br />Jean is experienced in strategic
  planning; board relations; master planning; operations and emergency response;
  fundraising; communications; civic partnerships; and program, education and
  exhibition development. With a keen ability to listen, communicate, and
  collaborate across diverse constituencies, she has held executive leadership
  positions in museums in both the U.S. and the U.K., and in government and
  education in the U.S. She views museums as modern-day public squares with the
  potential to bring people together across disciplines, boundaries, and
  disparities. Her passion - connecting people to the natural world - is driven
  by a core belief that people live happier, healthier lives when they connect
  with nature. <br /><br />Jean leads The Chicago Botanic Garden, one of the
  world's largest and most visited botanic gardens, welcoming more than 1.3
  million visitors annually while reaching tens of thousands more through its
  science, learning, and urban agriculture programs. Under her leadership, the
  Garden has expanded its reach through local, national and global programs that
  emphasize the importance of plant conservation to our well-being. She oversees
  a $65 million organization with a main campus of 385 acres, multiple urban
  farm sites, more than 700 staff, and a 65-member board. She has successfully
  delivered multiple capital projects, an overhaul of the Garden’s admission and
  membership structures, and a post-COVID recovery plan that grew attendance and
  revenue to record levels.<br /><br />Her notable achievements include delivery
  of record growth in earned revenue, completion of a $130 million capital
  campaign, expansion of the Garden’s flagship urban agriculture program, a new
  purpose and visual identity for the Garden, and advocating for the successful
  2022 VOTE YES referendum, securing stable funding for Cook County’s Forest
  Preserves.<br />Jean's extensive background in cultural institutions includes
  pivotal roles at the U.K’s Science Museum Group, where she served as Deputy
  Director of the London flagship Science Museum, and as Director of the Museum
  of Science and Industry in Manchester where she led a transformational
  turn-around. She was instrumental in managing multi-million-pound budgets,
  developing award-winning exhibitions, and fostering collaborations across
  disciplines in the Group’s four museums. <br /><br />As Vice President of
  Education and Guest Services at the Museum of Science and Industry in Chicago,
  she developed innovative educational initiatives and oversaw large operational
  teams. Earlier in her career, she held leadership roles in public policy and
  community service organizations, including serving as Chief of Staff for the
  Chicago Board of Education and Assistant to the Mayor of Chicago.
